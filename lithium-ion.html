Powertrain Architecture

Intelligent Power.
Precision Engineering.

At the heart of every JAC electric forklift is an advanced Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4) cell chemistry managed by intelligent real-time microcontrollers.

Integrated seamlessly with intelligent CAN-bus controllers, our lithium-ion technology delivers constant lifting torque and uncompromised duty cycles, eliminating the battery degradation typical of legacy lead-acid systems.

Global Component Standard

Engineered With Tier-1 Global Components

LFP Cell Chemistry

We exclusively deploy Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4) cells, providing the highest thermal runaway resistance, zero toxic gassing, and 3,000+ deep charge cycles.

Advanced Microcontrollers

High-speed intelligent motor controllers regulate hydraulic flow and regenerative braking to ensure millimetre precision during sensitive pallet placing.

Intelligent 24/7 BMS

Our integrated Battery Management System continuously monitors individual cell voltages, pack temperatures, and current draws, safeguarding your investment.

High-Density Pack

Lithium-Ion Battery Systems

Unlike traditional fuel or lead-acid trucks, JAC lithium systems maintain steady output throughout the entire discharge curve. Voltage does not sag when battery state-of-charge drops, ensuring your operators have 100% full lift speed from start to end of shift.

With zero water topping, zero equalization charges, and rapid opportunity charging, logistics facilities frequently reclaim 15–20 hours of maintenance labor per truck every month.

Strategic Advantages of Going Electric

01

Superior Energy ROI

Electricity costs per kilowatt-hour yield up to 65% operational savings compared to industrial diesel and LPG tank refills.

02

Rapid Opportunity Charging

Top up during 15-minute tea breaks or 45-minute shift handovers. Multi-shift operations run seamlessly on a single battery.

03

Maintenance-Free Powertrain

Zero engine belts, spark plugs, injectors, radiators, or oil filters. Greatly reduces unexpected mechanical breakdown risks.

04
