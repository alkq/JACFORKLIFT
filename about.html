Our Origins

A Global Brand, Reimagined for Singapore

JAC Material Handling is the exclusive distributor of JAC electric forklifts in Singapore. We specialize in zero-emission, energy-efficient equipment designed specifically for modern high-density logistics and humid industrial environments.

As part of Anhui Jianghuai Automobile Group Corp., Ltd. (JAC Motors), we deliver globally proven solutions adapted to regional compliance requirements. Founded in 1964 and listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange, JAC is a comprehensive manufacturing group producing commercial transports, advanced powertrains, and specialized logistics machinery.

Supported by 60+ years of engineering rigor, JAC units are constructed with state-of-the-art robotic welding, German-designed masts, and rigorous multi-stage quality inspections before arrival at our Singapore distribution hub.
