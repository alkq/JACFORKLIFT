Our Story

A Global Brand, Reimagined for Singapore

JAC Material Handling is the exclusive distributor of JAC electric forklifts in Singapore. The company specialises in energy-efficient and lithium-powered equipment designed for modern logistics and industrial operations.

As part of Anhui Jianghuai Automobile Group Corp., Ltd. (JAC), we deliver globally backed solutions adapted to local industry requirements. Founded in 1964 and listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange, JAC is a comprehensive automobile enterprise group with capabilities spanning commercial vehicles, passenger vehicles, and advanced powertrain development.

Supported by strong engineering expertise and advanced manufacturing capabilities, our range includes both indoor and heavy-duty electric forklifts, making efficient and reliable operations across various industries. Backed by over 60 years of forklift research and development, JAC Material Handling solutions are built with precision manufacturing, advanced production technology, and strict quality control systems.
